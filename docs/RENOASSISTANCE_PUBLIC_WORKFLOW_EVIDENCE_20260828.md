# RenoAssistance public workflow evidence — 2026-08-28

## Why this matters

RenoAssistance is currently one of the highest-leverage upstream ProjectPermit validation targets because it combines a pre-quote renovation intake workflow, a large contractor network, and explicit public responsibility for permit-related project support.

This note records public evidence only. It does **not** count as E2 volume evidence and does not change the Go/No-Go score by itself.

## Publicly observed workflow

RenoAssistance says homeowners describe their project first, then its team helps define needs/planning and matches the homeowner to up to three Verified Contractors for quotes.

Its commercial service pages explicitly describe the Account Manager's role as supporting the customer across **budgets, permits, deadlines, and quotes**. This places permit handling inside the same operational workflow where ProjectPermit would need to create value, rather than only after a permit application has already been filed.

Public residential material also describes planning/code-compliance assessment for larger renovation work before contractor execution.

## Public scale signals

Current public pages report:

- 2,400+ Verified Contractors in Quebec and Ontario;
- 137,000+ completed/supported residential projects/clients as a cumulative scale signal;
- 5,500+ completed commercial projects as a cumulative scale signal;
- more than $100M total value of projects completed during 2024;
- 1,249 clients represented in 2024 post-renovation satisfaction surveys.

The contractor page states that new matching projects enter continuously and that the top 10 contractors average more than $1M in annual project value.

## What this proves

The public evidence strengthens three claims:

1. **Workflow fit exists:** permit work is explicitly part of a real renovation-management workflow that also handles quotes and budgets.
2. **A potentially high-leverage buyer exists:** the platform already coordinates a large contractor network and substantial annual project value.
3. **The problem is not purely downstream filing:** RenoAssistance advertises permit support as part of broader project planning/management, before and during contractor execution.

## What this does NOT prove

Do not infer any of the following from the public evidence:

- current monthly permit-preflight candidate volume;
- the share of projects where permit applicability is unresolved at intake;
- that RenoAssistance would buy an external deterministic rules API;
- that its existing advisors lack sufficient internal permit knowledge;
- that $100M of annual project value maps directly to ProjectPermit calls;
- that cumulative project counts can be divided by company age to create a current monthly denominator.

Therefore this remains **workflow/scale support, not qualifying E2**.
