### Why PCC is now the cleanest first-pilot archetype

`Vancouver coverage + current project families + Jobber + quote/project workflow + explicit permit/regulation maintenance burden`

### Exact build-vs-buy question for a PCC-like buyer

The public evidence suggests a sharper question than generic permit pain:

> You already track municipal permit / zoning / bylaw changes. Looking at your last 20 estimates, how many required that knowledge before you could finalize scope, price or schedule — and which part of maintaining that knowledge would you actually externalize versus keep as internal judgement?

Then ask:

> If a maintained API supplied deterministic current facts + official evidence/change history, would you use it underneath your Jobber/project workflow while keeping final judgement in-house?

This directly tests ProjectPermit's moat.

### What remains unknown

Public sources do not establish:

- `last 20 estimates` denominator;
- time spent on research per estimate;
- how many Vancouver jobs versus other Lower Mainland municipalities;
- whether weekly monitoring is centralized in one employee or distributed;
- externalization preference;
- willingness to pay.

No E2 change.

### Why it matters

`Jobber job -> official regulatory/inspection/document portals -> inspection scheduling -> required documents -> job costing`

- larger installation/upgrade projects;
- possibly electrical/HVAC adjacency not yet represented in current families.

Therefore treat Ottawa Plumbing & Heating as an **operational workflow specimen**, not the primary current-family pilot.

## Hard anti-expansion rule

Do not add Richmond Hill, Kingston, Stirling-Rawdon, Coquitlam, Surrey, etc. merely because a compelling contractor operates there.

A new jurisdiction is justified only after a buyer in that jurisdiction provides:

1. recent bounded frequency;
2. material workflow consequence;
3. preference to externalize maintained regulatory work;
4. representative project-family fit;
5. plausible payment path.

Until then, validate on current-city records.
