# Soumissions.net Network Boundary — 2026-08-28

## Why this matters

Soumissions.net is a current Quebec multi-service quote platform that could look like evidence for a very large Oolong-operated aggregation network.

The public evidence is useful, but the ownership boundary must remain explicit.

## Current public scale / service context

The current site displays:

- **200,000+ soumissions**;
- **600+ partner professionals**;
- **300+ sites**;
- **100+ service categories**.

It also exposes renovation categories relevant or adjacent to current ProjectPermit families, including:

- home additions;
- adjacent/detached garages;
- sheds;
- kitchen cabinets/refacing;
- plumbers / water-heater work / bathroom work;
- basement finishing;
- other renovation work.

Source:

- `https://soumissions.net/`

These figures are public platform/network claims. They are not a recent monthly unique-project denominator and must not be converted into ProjectPermit calls.

## Oolong relationship

The current Soumissions.net footer states:

- `Création de Site Internet: Oolong Media`.

That is evidence of an Oolong web-development relationship.

However, the current Terms page reviewed does **not** identify Oolong Media Inc. as owner/data controller. It refers to the service as `Soumissions.net`, describes Soumissions.net as the intermediary, and provides `contact@soumissions.net` as the contact.

Source:

- `https://soumissions.net/termes-et-conditions/`

Therefore the current classification is:

- `Oolong-created / relationship verified`;
- `Oolong ownership/data-control unverified`.

Do **not** add Soumissions.net's 200k submissions or 300-site claim to an Oolong-owned denominator.

## Strategic interpretation

Soumissions.net strengthens the broader hypothesis that Quebec quote traffic is organized through large multi-service / multi-site networks rather than only isolated single-brand marketplaces.

But it does not strengthen the Oolong operator denominator until one of the following is verified:

1. Oolong owns or controls the relevant Soumissions.net lead data;
2. Oolong can integrate/enrich those leads through a common CRM/data path;
3. a current operator confirms that Soumissions.net is part of the same commercial lead network.

Until then, Soumissions.net remains a **network-structure lead**, not Oolong volume evidence.
