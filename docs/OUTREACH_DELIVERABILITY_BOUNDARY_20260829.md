## Evidence boundary

Spam placement, bounce status, no-bounce delivery and opens/clicks do not qualify as E2/E3/E4/E5.

A spam report means only:

- some cold-email response rate may be suppressed by deliverability friction;
- lack of reply is even less suitable as market evidence;
- expanding the number of cold messages to compensate would increase noise rather than evidence quality.

It does **not** mean:

- silent recipients would otherwise have replied positively;
- previous non-replies should be reclassified;
- the commercial score should increase;
- outreach volume should be increased.

## Operating rule

Until stronger external evidence appears:

1. **Do not launch another broad cold batch.** Ordinary marketplace/vendor discovery is already saturated.
2. Prioritize established human threads and bounded follow-ups that can upgrade evidence, such as SubmitX's current monthly-cadence question.
3. Prefer clearly published sales, business-development, research or partner inboxes over generic support when the question is commercial/architectural.
4. Do not guess email addresses or use privacy/legal contacts for product validation.
5. Do not resend a silent thread merely because the Gmail display name/icon changed.
6. Bounce and spam observations affect delivery confidence only; they never count as market negatives or positives.
7. A new cold contact is justified only when it is structurally different and directly score-moving: a new buyer class, representative-data route, or exact external machine-contract boundary.
8. Once a human has replied, keep follow-ups narrow. Ask the single missing denominator/workflow/build-vs-buy/resource question rather than restarting the pitch.
